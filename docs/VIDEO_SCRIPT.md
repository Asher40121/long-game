# 90-second walkthrough: script, shot list and recording checklist

Recorded by Phil with macOS screen recording (⌘ Shift 5). No other software needed.
About 215 spoken words, which comes to roughly 90 seconds at a relaxed pace.

## Script and shot list

| Time | On screen (what you click) | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | **Landing page** (`/long-game/`). Hold still; let the map preview show. | "I'm Phil Albritton. I've spent years working with toy, game and collectibles brands, and I've been a founder myself. In this category, the best acquisitions don't come off a banker's list. They come from relationships you keep warm for years. So I built Long Game." |
| 0:12–0:30 | Click **Relationships**. Slowly move the mouse along Martin Leclerc's row (Wildmoor), then point at the red bars on Walt Ashby and Pieter Halden. | "Each row is a founder. | 0:44–1:02 | Click **Emberline** (Dana Okafor). Point at the fit score (69, Strong), then scroll to Dana's founder card. | "Fit is measured against real gaps in our portfolio. Scroll slowly through the brief. | "One click gives the CFO a one-page brief: why it fits, the key risks, what we don't know yet, and a next step with an owner. Facts only. **If you run long:** cut the founder-card sentence in the Emberline segment (saves about 8 seconds).
**Keep it truthful:** every number above (69, two and a half years) is in the demo data. If you change the data, check the script.

## Before you record

- [ ] Open the **live demo** in Chrome: https://asher40121.github.io/long-game/
- [ ] Footer → **Reset demo data** → confirm, so dates are fresh and nothing is edited.
- [ ] Light mode (the half-circle button in the header).
- [ ] Use a clean Chrome window: **File → New Window**, hide the bookmarks bar (⌘ Shift B), no extra tabs.
- [ ] Browser zoom 110% (⌘ +) so text reads well on a phone.
- [ ] Turn on **Do Not Disturb** (Control Center → Focus) and quit Slack, Mail and Messages.
- [ ] Hide desktop icons if any part of the desktop will show.
- [ ] Quiet room. Wired earbuds with a mic, or the Mac's built-in mic about 18 inches away.
- [ ] Read the script out loud twice with a timer before recording.

## Recording (⌘ Shift 5)

- [ ] Press **⌘ Shift 5** and choose **Record Selected Portion**. Drag the box to cover just the Chrome window.
- [ ] **Options** → Microphone: pick your earbuds or "MacBook Microphone". Turn on **Show Mouse Clicks**.
- [ ] Click **Record**, wait 2 seconds, then start talking.
- [ ] Move the mouse slowly and deliberately; pause on each thing you name.
- [ ] Stop with the stop button in the menu bar. The file lands on your Desktop.

**Recording in pieces is fine.** Record each row of the table as its own clip, then join them in QuickTime:
open the first clip, **Edit → Add Clip to End**, pick the next one, and repeat.
Trim dead air at the start and end with **Edit → Trim** (⌘ T).

## Export and share

- [ ] QuickTime: **File → Export As → 1080p**.
- [ ] Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**. Title: "Long Game: a relationship-first deal pipeline (90-second demo)".
  Description: the demo link, the GitHub link, and "All companies, founders and numbers are fictional."
- [ ] Check YouTube's auto-captions and fix any mistakes (Subtitles → Edit).
- [ ] Send Claude the link. It goes at the top of the README and on the landing page as a **plain link, not an embed**:
  an embedded YouTube player would load Google tracking on the demo, which breaks the "no tracking" promise.
